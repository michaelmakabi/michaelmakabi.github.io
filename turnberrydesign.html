19707 TURNBERRY WAY · UNIT 17D · AVENTURA, FL

WHOLE

Whole Home

Design language: warm modern minimalism — a neutral palette of beige, cream and sand, light oak, and lots of natural light.

  • Paint touch-ups throughout the home
  • Clean and repaint all marked areas
  • Fix gaps at the doors
  • Sand and paint the spackle patch by the switches to match the wall
  • Finish the wood/marble floor transition flush and close the gap at the jamb
  • Caulk and paint the baseboard-to-wall seam
  • Clean and touch up the paint around the Legrand switch plates
ENTRY

Entry

Warm cream walls and the glossy white marble floor, same as the living room.

  • Black front door with a smart lock and gold handle
  • Floor-to-ceiling light-oak built-in closet with vent slots at the bottom
  • Cover the electrical panel fully with a larger picture (kept easily removable for emergency access)
  • Move the LIRON / MICHAEL (EST. 2023) sign picture to another spot in the home
HALLWAY

Hallway

A minimal, empty look — clean white walls, the same glossy white marble floor as the living room, no clutter.

  • Light-oak doors and frames
  • Matte black door handles
  • Rectangular black-framed ceiling light
  • Close and finish the open recessed ceiling light, with drywall and paint around it
  • Cover or replace the exposed detector base on the ceiling
  • Repair and paint the corner wall (spackle marks and hairline crack)
  • Finish the cleaning-supplies closet with a matching oak door and frame paint
  • Organize the closet interior: top shelf, tray and basket for supplies and cloths, wall mounts for mop and broom, hanging/charging dock for the cordless vacuum
  • Install an electrical outlet inside the cleaning closet for the vacuum charging dock
LIVING

Living Room

Warm beige / cream / sand palette, light oak, glossy white marble floor. Greenery is the only color accent in the space.

  • Hang the large dark-framed mirror on the column wall between kitchen and living room
  • Replace the white TV console with a floating light-oak console
  • Hide all TV and console cables, including the outlet above the TV
  • Finish the wall opening under the TV: recessed outlet/cable plate, drywall finish and paint, TV cable routed inside the wall
  • Dining corner: clear the dining table of daily clutter — travertine bowl centerpiece only

  • Large light-beige fabric sectional with matching cushions and a throw
  • Light-wood rattan / rope armchair
  • Round travertine / beige stone coffee table
  • Large rug in beige–sand tones
  • Open light-oak bookshelf for the corner, with the Ten Commandments tablets as a decorative piece above it
  • Double curtains — white sheer + heavy beige at the sides, floor-to-ceiling
  • Greenery — the only color accent in the space
  • ✓Oval light-oak dining table — fluted pedestal base, glass top
  • ✓Beige upholstered dining chairs with matching cushions (set)
MASTER

Master Bedroom

Light-oak wood floor (not marble — different from the living room), fluted oak, warm cream and taupe tones. In the corner of the room: a baby corner inside the parents' room.

  • Light-oak wood floor (not marble — different from the living room)
  • Wide fluted-oak headboard
  • Install the light-oak sliding door on the wardrobe opening (top track cover, recessed pull)
  • Finish the wardrobe door frame and repair the damaged baseboard at the frame
  • Sort and organize the wardrobe: clothes and shoes — donate, discard, arrange
  • Repair the baseboard and paint at the door jamb and finish the caulk line along the baseboard
  • Sand and paint the large spackle patch on the wall between the doors to match the wall
  • Decide: one wide dresser (63in) or two side by side under the master TV

  • Cream upholstered bed, cream bedding and a taupe throw
  • Pair of matching fluted nightstands
  • Pair of warm mushroom lamps for the nightstands
  • Fluted TV dresser / console
  • Large plush beige rug
  • Sheer curtains for the balcony door
  • Fluted changing dresser (baby corner)
  • Oval wooden crib + mattress + fitted sheets (baby corner)
  • Changing pad for the changing dresser (baby corner)
  • Mobile above the crib (baby corner)
  • Cream boucle nursing chair by the window + small side table (baby corner)
