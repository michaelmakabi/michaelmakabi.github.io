The Story

About Michael

The five domains where Michael builds, invests, and operates — and the record behind them.

Michael Makabi is a veteran real estate entrepreneur, investor, technologist, and multi-business founder with more than two decades of experience across residential, commercial, and off-market real estate.

He began his career in 2004 as a commercial realtor and quickly rose to become a licensed broker. Shortly after, Michael emerged as one of the early pioneers of commercial real estate wholesaling in the highly competitive Downtown Los Angeles (DTLA) market.

“One of the early pioneers of commercial real estate wholesaling in DTLA.”Downtown Los Angeles · Since 2004

Over more than 20 years, he has evolved into a nationally recognized operator, syndicator, and investor — building, scaling, and leading companies at the intersection of real estate, technology, AI, and capital markets.

“Building, scaling, and leading companies at the intersection of real estate, technology, AI, and capital markets.”Operator · Syndicator · Investor

That intersection is the through-line of everything below: five disciplines, eleven companies, and one operating principle — make the impossible possible.

Inspiration

Principles

Reflections and principles from Michael — the mindset behind two decades of building.

PRINCIPLE 01

Be the Warrior of Your Life

I am the warrior of my life. My intuition and foresight surmount all. It is in my nature to be kind, strong, loving, & loyal. But know this; when it comes to matters of protecting my family, my business, and my trusted inner circle, do not trifle with me; Don’t ever mistake my silence for ignorance, my calmness for acceptance or my kindness for weakness. For I am the most powerful and relentless creature you’ll ever come to know.

PRINCIPLE 02

What is Happiness to You?

I’ve come to a simple revelation. Happiness is owning up to who you are, facing the music, knowing your self worth, trusting your truth, surrounding yourself with people of genuine substance and added value, sharing a masterful vision, having the guts to charter a course to set off through this amazing adventure called life to triumph all of the puzzling obstacles it throws your way and to know you have what it takes to surmount it all… no matter how challenging the odds are. Always remember that your happiness and reality is what you choose to make it. The rest is just white noise.

PRINCIPLE 03

The Hell with Circumstances — I Make My Own Luck (מַזָל)

Luck in Hebrew translates to the word MAZAL (מַ-זָ-ל). The very definition means to be at the right place (מקום), at the right time (זְמַן) and to seize the opportunity that is presented to you (ללכת על זה). The universe doesn’t send you into a circumstance unless it knows you’re ready to accept the challenge of that opportunity. The success that will come from your decisive initiative and action is waiting for you to take control and become a Creator. To become the Cause not just the mere Effect of your life. Are you strong enough to stare your opponents in the eyes and win your victory?

PRINCIPLE 04

Change the World with Your Vision

Don’t underestimate the power of your vision to change the world. Whether that world is your office, your community, an industry or a global movement, you need to have a core belief that what you contribute can fundamentally change the paradigm or way of thinking about problems.
